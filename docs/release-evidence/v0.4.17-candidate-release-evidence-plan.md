## Candidate feature acceptance focus

The 0.4.17 candidate integrates money answers, assistant instructions,
workspace preservation, truthful sync labels and update recovery. Acceptance
must exercise the final integrated behavior, not superseded lane proposals:

1. **Money figures and attribution.** Verify evidenced own-account transfers,
   retirement/investment withdrawals and qualifying loan proceeds are excluded
   from the lead, with the Note and exact inclusion phrases restoring the
   appropriate view. Uncertain pairs, outside-party receipts and repayments
   must retain their correct treatment. Preserve gross rows and stored data.
   Cash flow must return `cash_flow_unsupported` without a figure or model
   fallback. Profit/net income stays labelled as money in minus money out,
   not accounting profit or cash flow. Check payer evidence, ambiguous merchant
   selectors, independent coverage and cross-format import overlap refusals.
2. **Assistant rules and search.** Both assistants must receive money rules v3
   and preserve the returned figures, Note, scope, citations and confidence.
   Exercise search-first document lookup, explicit empty results, unsupported
   answers with real candidates and incomplete search. Remote search promises
   no unsupported date filter, sort or globally newest record. Raw error bodies
   must stay out of replies, and retrieved text remains data, not instructions.
3. **Workspace files.** Verify owned CLAUDE.md/AGENTS.md setup and refresh,
   My accounts data preservation, guarded missing-file creation, and the
   session-folder nudge. Exercise foreign ownership, redirected paths,
   ambiguous markers, concurrent edits, write failures and retained recovery
   files. A refused or interrupted write must not be called successful.
4. **Sync status.** A certified quiet check says checked, no changes and does
   not invent accepted work. Unscheduled uploads remain unwatched snapshots,
   retired uploads remain historical, and genuine failures, incomplete work
   and safety pauses remain visible. Check cadence units and stuck-run advice.
5. **Update recovery.** Exercise pause/resume with queued work, durable
   recovery fences, daily restoration failures, pre-mutation rollback and
   required acceptance failures. Verify schedule inventory and exact readback,
   preservation of unrelated crons, bounded native scheduler calls and literal
   Windows restart commands. A failed required restore or acceptance step must
   not claim ordinary completion.
