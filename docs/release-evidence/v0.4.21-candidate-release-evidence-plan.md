## Candidate feature acceptance focus

The 0.4.21 candidate contains the bounded money, daily refresh, provider
transition and recovery paths below. Acceptance must exercise the final
integrated behavior, not superseded lane proposals:

1. **Everyday-money questions.** Run the real Worker question and answer path
   for account and label balances, current and previous month inflow and
   outflow, vendor spending, recent card activity and largest expenses. Prove
   owner-local calendar windows, exact account scope and refusal of unsupported
   or ambiguous selectors.
2. **Transfers and incomplete evidence.** Prove supported own-account USD pairs
   are excluded from inflow and outflow while non-USD pairs remain visible as a
   known limit. Exercise partial rankings and exact-plus-possible vendor rows,
   and require a structured gap before any incomplete result can look complete.
3. **Durable daily causes.** Exercise calendar-only, credential-only, mixed and
   completed controls through the real daily status and source inventory
   renderers. Each arm must reach its inventory and rendering decision, and the
   owner lines must not collapse to one uniform result.
4. **macOS lifecycle wait.** With an injected clock and lock adapter, prove the
   daily runner retries only on macOS for at most 55 seconds, records the wait
   in the terminal receipt and preserves the one-attempt contract elsewhere.
5. **Bank-provider transition.** Run preview and apply through the real command
   boundary. Bind approval to inspected state, refuse an active connection or
   ledger history before a write, prove an atomic manifest replacement and show
   the remaining deployment and connection work without implying it occurred.
6. **Bounded recovery cause.** Prove daily-update recovery persists its recovery
   state before rendering one allowlisted cause. Unknown and unsafe material
   must stay hidden while the receipt and retry guidance remain available.
7. **Legacy macOS scheduler.** From exact installed fixture bytes, prove update
   validates, captures, pauses and restores only the identity-owned eligible
   scheduler, with unchanged normal status checks and closed refusal controls.
8. **Integrated regression.** Repeat current-version, package privacy, update
   audit and relevant money, daily, provider and recovery suites. Keep every
   negative control paired with a decision-point assertion and a green control.

## Known issues and supervised-update precautions

- A failed bank-document replacement after a committed import or account change
  can leave stale bank documents until the scheduled retry.
- Own transfers between non-USD accounts can remain in money-in or money-out
  figures.
- Do not switch bank providers while a bank connection is in progress. A
  request already crossing the transition can write after the transition
  clears.
- A very large ledger can return `query too large` instead of an answer.
- This candidate has no account-link recovery command.
- A Windows daily refresh still skips the day when its lifecycle lock is busy.
- The local bank-provider manifest validator does not enforce every schema
  string format.
- After a failed `brain update`, run `brain health`. The inherited
  pause-ambiguity and unconfirmed-migration messages still need verification
  against the exact sealed package rather than carrying forward earlier proof.
- Verify that a private rollback bookmark receipt was durably saved before
  deployment or migration in the separately authorized update campaign.
  Interrupted-update recovery still needs its own acceptance evidence.
- Review money totals against cited rows, dates, currency, account coverage,
  possible transfers and visible gaps. This planning record authorizes no live
  update and provides no field proof.
