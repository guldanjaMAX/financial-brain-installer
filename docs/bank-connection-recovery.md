# Recover unreadable bank access while keeping history

This candidate feature requires the reviewed update and owner approval before live use. It applies to any supported bank whose saved access produces the exact unreadable-reference diagnosis. A missing key, a readable connection, a removed connection or ordinary expired bank permission needs its own recovery check.

The installer can run `brain bank custody <manifest> [--json]` for a read-only check. It reports each active connection's declared key version, current dedicated wrapping-key fingerprint, whether saved access opens, last good sync observation and saved-cursor fingerprint. Legacy authentication keys are never fingerprinted. Their identity is reported as unavailable. Neither fingerprints nor successful opening prove complete bank history. Exit 0 means all checked references open, 2 means an unreadable reference, 3 means another custody diagnostic requires review; command or transport failures use the normal CLI failure exit.

1. In Access > Banks, choose **Replace unreadable connection** or **Continue recovery** for the affected bank. Keep the saved accounts and history. Do not use Disconnect or start an unrelated fresh connection.
2. Read the preservation preview. After the installer establishes that original access cannot be recovered, confirm that check. Complete the bank sign-in yourself on the provider's screen.
3. Fetch the next history page until the account and overlap review appears. The new connection uses its own cursor. The old cursor and older months remain saved.
4. Confirm each account's name, last four, type, currency and owner. Similar last four digits alone are insufficient. A missing or ambiguous account stays in review; the software does not guess.
5. Compare uncertain occurrences against your statement. Two identical purchases can be distinct. A duplicate, pending-to-posted change, correction or withdrawal needs its exact saved occurrence and supporting evidence. If an already withdrawn occurrence is delivered again, select **Withdrawn, including a redelivery that stays withdrawn**, choose that saved occurrence and record the statement evidence. Confirming adds a withdrawal to its history without adding money. A newer modified provider record and explicit correction are required to reinstate it. Uncertain rows remain visible as open review items with their affected dollars. They keep affected answers below High while sync finishes and later proved transactions continue to arrive. No uncertain row enters the counted ledger.
6. Check the choices, then confirm handover. Before that single database transaction, answers retain the old ledger. After it, the same logical accounts include the proved gap activity once. The old connection reads **Replaced, history kept**. Retiring it locally does not revoke its old permission at the bank.
7. Compare one complete earlier month and the break/recovery period with statements, per account, currency and direction. Missing dollars remain unknown until measured. A sign-in success or arriving webhook does not prove complete coverage.
8. Reload or continue the same recovery after interruption. If the sign-in outcome is uncertain, preserve that operation and ask the installer to recover its receipt. Do not exchange the handoff again or start another sign-in. Cancellation before exchange keeps the old ledger. Candidate custody after exchange requires separate review and consent cleanup.
9. If needed, review rollback from the recovery page, then confirm. This points the accounts back to the old connection and retains newly proved financial facts. It does not make the unreadable old access usable. Known unresolved dollars stay visible in answers and in the recovery review. To continue, review and confirm resuming this recovery, then separately confirm handover. Resuming alone keeps the old connection selected and publishes no new activity.

Answers still lead with the figure first and the period in words, name the covered accounts and dates, state missing or possibly duplicated dollars (unknown when unmeasured), label likely estimates with their basis, and say how to check. Every answer keeps its data-computed score internal and hides confidence labels and percentages from owners. Thin or missing evidence gets one plain sentence without a number or scale word; preserve why and what is missing in the five answer parts. Unresolved affected recovery periods cannot earn High confidence. A recovery error must not replace a supported money answer.

An occurrence keeps one history through account moves, pending-to-posted changes and later connection replacements. A withdrawal of an older provider ID follows that history to the current saved occurrence. During review, answers disclose the dollars and dates throughout that lineage, including its former accounts. Once the exact withdrawal is confirmed, totals exclude it and the saved evidence remains available. Owner review settles the saved occurrence independently of sync completion. If a posted observation and removal of its pending observation arrive together, both remain in history and only the posted occurrence counts. The pending removal never withdraws that posted money. A posting that changes accounts still offers its known pending predecessor for review.

This is offline candidate guidance, not release evidence or permission to reconnect a live account.
