This record answers a narrower question than the incident ledger: which known
incidents can affect the exact Monday pilot? It does not change an incident's
canonical status, convert local tests into field proof, or authorize a provider,
Brain, customer, credential, deployment, release, or publication action.

The central wants-versus-needs rule is:

- A safety or truth fix is a need whenever its feature is exercised.
- The pilot may defer the feature and record the incident as not applicable.
- Deferring a feature is not closing its incidents. Those incidents become
  release gates before that feature is advertised or used.

## Decision vocabulary

- **Applicable, pending**: Monday exercises the affected path. The named exact
  package, CI, disposable, Mac, or physical Windows evidence must pass before
  the relevant rung proceeds.
- **Applicable, inherited control**: prior field evidence exists, but the exact
  candidate still has to retain the control through package and CI checks.
- **Not applicable**: the frozen pilot does not exercise or advertise the
  affected capability. Encountering that state stops the pilot rather than
  silently broadening scope.

   in a dedicated source root; and
5. prove the same item is received, saved, searchable, correctly cited, and
   refused when the question asks for a fact or scope the evidence cannot prove.

The incidents marked not applicable are hard exclusions, not casual omissions.
If the pilot encounters or starts advertising passkeys, Plaid, Google on
Windows, owner upload/private links, ARM64, 0.3.x upgrade, linked-folder ingest,
mixed-source grants, or a paginated financial provider, it stops and the
corresponding incident becomes an applicable blocker.

This disposition records required boundaries only. It does not prove that the
local candidate implements them, that any related test passes, or that the
package, CI, physical Windows, Cloudflare, internal-owner, or live-Brain rung is
ready. The teardown path remains separately held and still requires
exact-candidate Cloudflare evidence, explicit source and target approvals, and
supervised live execution. The earlier one-campaign manual exception is
superseded and was never approved. UPDATE-043 also needs explicit acceptance
that the affected legacy Wrangler compatibility route is excluded from every
Monday rung.
