neither prior-version deferral and does not change either incident's status or
acceptance criteria.

  DPAPI, browser login, interrupted-update, clean-exit, or printed-command

  ARM64 evidence, the row stays open and held. Windows x64 remains the only

  held. Bank breadth ships unproven in 0.4.23, and bank invitations stay
  closed. ADR 008 defines the narrowed scope.

The release note keeps both limitations visible to the owner. No deferral is
renewed by this plan.

## Candidate feature acceptance focus

The 0.4.23 candidate contains the integrated changes since sealed 0.4.22.
Acceptance must exercise final behavior rather than superseded lane proposals:

1. **Account-kind review and repayments.** Through the real owner review and
   ledger paths, confirm supported unknown accounts, preserve provider facts,
   reject a known loan being reclassified as cash, and retain uncertainty for
   unresolved liabilities or conflicting deposit evidence. Verify migration
   0058 and its exact readback. Review is not account-link or coverage proof.
2. **Expense periods and ranking.** Prove one eligibility rule precedes top-N
   ranking, repayments remain separate, and posted-record disclosure is visible.
   Exercise undated early-month and empty-month questions with covered prior
   months; current-period coverage warnings must survive compact rendering.
   Explicit dates must keep their requested period.
3. **Inflow and transfer uniqueness.** Show recorded inflow, matched internal
   pairs and outside inflow. Decide uniqueness before filtering, consume each
   row once, and leave ambiguous or unsupported pairs unmatched. Prove account,
   currency and coverage limits remain visible.
4. **Other money routes and veto.** Exercise balance aliases, owner-local month
   grammar, near-date payments, recurring monthly charges and unsupported
   forecasts. Exclude future or malformed balance observations before ranking.
   A generic transcript statement must not trigger the money veto; real money
   claims must retain it. Supported expense questions are green controls.
5. **Scheduler rebinding.** Inventory every supported owned scheduler, pause
   and rebind to the installed runtime, and read back exact definitions and
   enablement. Prove no data-writing job starts during rebinding, disabled jobs
   stay disabled, foreign definitions are refused and partial failure remains
   recoverable without completing the update prematurely.
6. **Curated readback.** Confirm exact posted document identities without a
   full inventory walk. Exercise failed indexed readback and safe diagnostic
   codes. Explicit audit mode must retain its inventory responsibility.
7. **Meeting export.** Exercise the declared Granola file-export source through
   common ingest, including notes, summaries, transcripts, revision changes,
   duplicates, conflicts and item receipts. Refuse linked roots and members,
   while allowing the reviewed canonical temporary-directory behavior.
8. **Small explicit deletions.** Prove exact per-item deletion causes survive
   fingerprinting and duplicate observations. Automatic Gmail and Calendar
   application requires both count thresholds and exact approval/readback.
   Drive, missing evidence, conflicting causes, exclusions and larger plans
   remain held. Every refusal must reach a nonempty decision with a green peer.
9. **Integrated regression and replay.** Repeat current-version, package
   privacy, update audit and all affected suites. Offline replay must retain
   historical inputs and independent expectations, reject stale dependency
   tapes and SQL, and preserve discriminating controls. A private exported
   ledger is instance material, outside the checkout and package. Synthetic
   replay and the prior host chain do not certify live answer correctness.

## Known issues and supervised-update precautions

- A failed bank-document replacement after a committed import or account change
  can leave stale bank documents until the scheduled retry.
- Own transfers between non-USD accounts can remain in money-in or money-out
  figures.
- Do not switch bank providers while a bank connection is in progress. A
  request already crossing the transition can write after the transition
  clears.
- A very large ledger can return `query too large` instead of an answer.
- This candidate has no account-link recovery command.
- A Windows daily refresh still skips the day when its lifecycle lock is busy.
- The local bank-provider manifest validator does not enforce every schema
  string format.
- After a failed `brain update`, run `brain health`. The inherited
  pause-ambiguity and unconfirmed-migration messages still need verification
  against the exact sealed package rather than carrying forward earlier proof.
- Verify that a private rollback bookmark receipt was durably saved before
  deployment or migration in the separately authorized update campaign.
  Interrupted-update recovery still needs its own acceptance evidence.
- Review money totals against cited rows, dates, currency, account coverage,
  possible transfers and visible gaps. This planning record authorizes no live
  update and provides no field proof.
