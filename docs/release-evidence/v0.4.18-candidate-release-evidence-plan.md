not change either incident's status or acceptance criteria.

  unavailable. Hosted x64 CI cannot supply the packaged-launch, DPAPI, browser

## Candidate feature acceptance focus

The 0.4.18 candidate integrates answer-trust, refusal, intent,
long-document, health, first-hour and calendar fixes. Acceptance must exercise
the final integrated behavior, not superseded lane proposals:

1. **Scanned and native evidence.** Verify only complete OCR receipts with no
   unreadable relied-on passage can support an answer, and that the answer and
   citation label the scanned basis. Prove stored labels cannot forge authority.
   Check bounded legacy native-text assessment and signed-order precedence over
   drafts without promoting notes, correspondence or incomplete extraction.
2. **Question and refusal gates.** Verify money checks do not veto unrelated
   questions, one missing-citation retry and one unreadable-verifier retry are
   bounded, and final refusals preserve records while naming `Why:` and `Next:`.
   A genuine retrieval failure must retain its failure status.
3. **Time and tax intent.** Exercise owner-zone relative windows, explicit
   years, caller-bound precedence, reliable-date filtering and empty-window
   honesty. Verify owner tax wording resolves only with supported filer, year
   and form evidence and does not adopt another filer or business return.
4. **First-hour retrieval.** Start keyword work before embedding, then prove a
   cited keyword answer can remain supported while the meaning index catches
   up. The exact meaning-search count line must be visible without a fabricated
   percentage. Provider failure, absent embedding and count mismatch must not be
   relabelled as ordinary indexing.
5. **Health and setup.** Make schema and source-state reads fail independently.
   Health must stay reachable with `ok:false` and `storage-unavailable`, and
   setup must refuse before cutover. Healthy, paused and non-D1 controls must
   retain their existing behavior.
6. **Long-document fetch.** Reconstruct a document across exact offset pages,
   reach its final section, preserve metadata and Unicode boundaries, and
   refuse invalid or past-end offsets after reaching the decision point.
7. **Calendar passcodes.** Keep a synthetic Zoom event while removing its
   passcode from indexed text. Prove the event remains searchable and the
   passcode does not survive. Query-string credential scanning must recognize
   the reviewed dotted passcode keys without broadening unrelated policy.
