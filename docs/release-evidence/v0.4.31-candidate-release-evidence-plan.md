## Owner answer acceptance

Every owner question must receive the best answer supported by the available
records. A refusal or a wrong figure at High confidence blocks release.
Internally, every answer is still computed with a confidence level from the
data and the assistant may only lower it; owners no longer see the word or
score (0.4.30 change). Below full support, the answer explains why and names
the missing evidence. Check the five parts: the figure and period first,
account and date coverage, missing or double-counted dollars, likely estimates
and their basis, and how to check.

Verify on the exact final package before claiming field acceptance: an update
from 0.4.26 and from 0.4.29 that applies the migrations in order through 0070,
`brain clean` on a small list, the update self-check report, the new setup,
app opening, Ask and bank pages, and the Gmail, Drive and calendar source
repairs.

Known limits remain visible: rolling back an empty partial replacement poll
can leave its resume action unavailable (review B08); an older Brain command
that still uses the shared Windows lock folder is not excluded by the private
per-user lease; SimpleFIN evening purchases may land on the next UTC day;
acceptance trusts the Worker's own explanation fields. Those explanations are
not independent proof of correctness. Audit each supported figure against
independent truth.
