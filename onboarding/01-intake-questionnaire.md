# Financial Brain intake: a short private worksheet

Use this only if you prefer a written worksheet. The guided interview in
`00-pre-install-interview.md` can collect the same information one question at
a time. You can leave anything blank. Unknown, unavailable, and not applicable
are useful answers and should not be replaced with guesses.

This worksheet does not authorize a login, connection, upload, OCR run,
deployment, billing change, passkey ceremony, or access grant.

---

## 1. What would help first?

**1.1** What would you most like your Financial Brain to help you understand?

**1.2** Is the picture mainly personal, household, business, or a combination?

**1.3** Which people, businesses, trusts, properties, or other entities do you
already know belong in that picture?

**1.4** Which years or periods matter most right now?

Anything named here begins as owner-stated. A possible mention in a document
does not become a confirmed entity, account, ownership relationship, or tax
obligation without evidence and owner review.

Examples of source types include email, a file store, accounting software,
payroll, bank or investment feeds, tax files, meeting transcripts, messages,
calendar, portal exports, and paper records. These are prompts, not a checklist
of things the owner must connect.

For each applicable person or entity, note whether the map appears to cover the
relevant accounts, books, payroll, tax returns, filings, and payments. Keep
"not applicable," "unknown," and "unavailable" separate.

---

## 3. Exclusions and access boundaries

**3.1** What accounts, folders, mailboxes, topics, or custodians must stay out?

**3.2** Is any material controlled by someone else or subject to special
confidentiality?

**3.3** Besides the owner, who may eventually ask questions, and which whole
sources should each person be allowed to use?

Financial Brain supports multiple source-level access zones. Each registered
source can have at most one zone label. A named grant can include one or more
allowed zones. An unzoned source remains owner-only and is excluded from every
named zone grant. A zone cannot separate individual items inside one source,
so a source with mixed audiences stays owner-only until a safe separate scope
is available or the owner deliberately chooses a broader boundary.

---

## 4. Coverage and reading quality

**4.1** What is the newest item that should be present?

**4.2** What is the oldest item that still matters?

**4.3** Are there a few specific records the owner would be worried not to
find?

**4.4** Which internal nicknames, project codes, or abbreviations should be
understood?

**4.5** Which records may be scanned, photographed, faxed, or handwritten?

OCR for scanned PDFs is available but off by default. It runs only when the
owner explicitly enables it. Before an OCR-enabled load, review the estimated
page count, cost range, time range, and daily spend cap. Machine-read text stays
marked as OCR and low-quality readings may be refused. A record with no usable
text is not automatically a scan; when the evidence cannot establish why, its
state remains unknown.

## 6. Computer and account readiness

**6.1** Which Mac or Windows computer will run setup and Claude Code?

**6.2** Does the owner have Claude Code installed and signed in on that
computer?

**6.3** Does the owner control the Cloudflare account that will hold the Brain?

The guided `brain tools` and packaged preflight checks handle the technical
details, including Node.js 22 or newer, at least 2 GiB free on the actual
install drive, a normal non-elevated terminal, Claude Code, Claude sign-in, the
technician skill, and pinned Wrangler. The owner separately confirms Workers
Paid on the exact Cloudflare account because the narrow sign-in cannot read
billing status.

On a new or replacement computer, begin read-only and verify three local pieces
separately: the exact reviewed Brain CLI, the `financial-brain-technician`
skill, and the owner-assistant MCP registration, which is the private local
connection between Claude Code and the Brain. Missing is different from broken.
Optimize may report which local pieces are missing or outdated, but it does not
install them or review passkeys and devices. Any supported repair is a separate
previewed action that the owner approves.

When browser control is available, Claude Code should offer to navigate
official pages and complete safe non-secret fields. It must return control to
the owner before login, 2FA, credential or secret entry, consent, billing, and
every secure passkey window.

---

## 7. Before the session ends

The owner and technician choose one next action together. The owner should not
receive a batch of unfamiliar tasks. If another person controls a needed
account or export, record that dependency without contacting them or pretending
the source is available.
