## Candidate feature acceptance focus

The 0.4.27 candidate combines the S10 integration with the four intended
folds only after their integration and review. The owner release-note draft
supplies the acceptance scope; it does not supply release evidence.

1. **Latest payments and balances.** Verify the newest posted matching date,
   ties on that date, every selected balance account, account status and pending
   observations. A matching deposit must not become proof of who paid.
2. **Merchant, calendar and account scope.** Keep unproved merchant identities
   separate, preserve confirmed business/personal account restrictions and
   refuse unsupported or conflicting date constraints. Missing coverage and
   unresolved account selection must withhold unsupported figures. Negative
   controls must reach their decision points beside successful controls.
3. **Money membership and refunds.** Repeat ordinary money-in/out, reciprocal
   transfers, confirmed-category spending and listed recurring exclusions on
   the final folds. Card payments, loan payments and owned movements must keep
   their disclosed exclusions. Verify refund disclosures on net-spending,
   card-description and monthly paths before making final acceptance claims.
4. **Keyword search and bank gaps.** Exercise the smaller keyword first pass
   and full-query fallback on a large synthetic collection. Verify SimpleFIN
   bank errors, loading history, unconfirmed account kinds and expense rankings
   that exclude loan activity and confirmed repayments while retaining card
   purchases.
5. **Windows lock and update path.** Exercise bounded lock-release retries and
   competing operation ownership. Separately prove the exact packaged update
   journeys from 0.4.8 and 0.4.24. Eligibility is pending; the 0.4.26 package is
   not the Windows fallback. A migration-only rehearsal is not an end-to-end
   owner update receipt.
6. **Integrated regression and privacy.** Repeat current-version, owner-rule
   hash, package privacy, update audit and every affected suite. Require all
   six platform/Node CI jobs on the final source. Synthetic answer checks and
   prior integration results do not certify broad live answer correctness.
   Private replay inputs stay outside product source and the package.

## Known issues and supervised-update precautions

- A question covering more than about 5,000 transactions can refuse, including
  recurring questions across several months. Try a shorter period or one
  account; a refusal is not proof of zero activity.
- Overlapping old-provider and SimpleFIN account history can double-count
  activity. Check one provider's account and period and have overlap reviewed
  without removing history.
- Coverage and observation dates use UTC and can appear one local day ahead.
- Possessive account wording and recurring cadence remain outside scope.
- Money-in/out, transfers and recurring lists still need final release
  acceptance; bounded synthetic results do not establish complete correctness.
- Refund-disclosure acceptance on net-spending, card-description and monthly
  paths remains pending until the final disclosure fold is reviewed.
- Windows update eligibility from 0.4.8 and 0.4.24, final Node 22 integration,
  six green CI jobs, native keyring/signing, live bank acceptance and the
  end-to-end owner update rehearsal remain pending.
- After an authorized update failure, check health and retain the private
  recovery record. Never restore a bookmark first or clear drain mode by hand.
